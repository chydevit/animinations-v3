## 0. Tag legend

| Tag | Meaning | How to narrate |
|---|---|---|
| **[DOCUMENTED – source, year]** | Stated in a primary written source. Give its date. | "According to …" |
| **[INSCRIPTION]** | Stated in a stone inscription (K. number). | "An inscription says …" |
| **[ARCHAEOLOGY]** | Excavated material, with its dating method. | "Archaeologists have found …" |
| **[LEGEND]** | Origin story or folk tale. Not history. | "A legend tells …" |
| **[DEBATED]** | Historians disagree. Give the positions and their authors. | "Historians still debate …" |
| **[HYPOTHESIS]** | A reasonable suggestion without firm proof. | "Some historians think …" |
| **[UNVERIFIED – do not narrate]** | A popular claim not confirmed in a primary source or solid scholarship. | Leave it out. |

**Access notes:** list what was read in full, and what is known only second-hand.
