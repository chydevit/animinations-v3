# Accuracy rules

The user wants accurate, neutral, respectful Cambodian history. These rules are not optional.

1. **No fact without a source.** Every narrated claim must appear in docs/fact_sheet.md with a tag and a citation.
   If a claim cannot be verified, it is tagged [UNVERIFIED – do not narrate] and left out.
2. **Tag every claim:**
   DOCUMENTED (written source, with its date) · INSCRIPTION (K. number) · ARCHAEOLOGY (with the dating method) ·
   LEGEND · DEBATED (name the positions) · HYPOTHESIS.
3. **Say the tag aloud.** "A legend tells…", "Chinese records say…", "Historians still debate…". Numbers in old
   chronicles are given as "the record says", never as plain fact.
4. **Badge every non-evidence shot.** recon, legend, map_recon, schematic, comparison (an object from another place
   or period), guide. Authentic images carry photo, artifact, inscription or satellite, plus a credit line.
5. **Maps:** ancient extents are soft zones ("blob"), never hard borders. Modern borders appear only faintly, for
   orientation. Positions are marked approximate.
6. **Contested topics** (border disputes, the Khmer Rouge, ethnic history): give each side's claim, attributed to its
   source. Keep a neutral tone and use no loaded adjectives.
7. **Images:** use only free licences (PD, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA), checked on the source page. Never use a photo of a
   similar object as if it were the real one: label it "comparison".
8. **Khmer text:** machine-written Khmer always needs review by a native-speaking historian. Say so in the hand-off
   and in the credits.
9. **DO-NOT-SAY list:** every fact sheet ends with the myths found during research and the reason each one is wrong.
