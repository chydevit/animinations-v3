# Music and sound plan

**Instruments.** The whole score is original and synthesised in `engine/audio.py`, using the khmer-2d-animation skill's pinpeat-inspired instrument set:
- roneat (xylophone)
- kong (gong circle)
- khloy (flute)
- tro (fiddle)
- chapei (lute)
- skor (drum)
- ching (small cymbals)
- a soft string-like pad for the "cinematic" bed

Every chapter has its own short **motif**: a six-note pentatonic cell. The motif is stated, varied (tail inverted, raised a step) and allowed to breathe between phrases.

**Mix.**
- Music is ducked by about 11 dB under any voice and mixed well below the narration.
- Ambience is ducked by about 7 dB.
- The master is normalised to −16 LUFS.

**Who plays Funan's music?** The film says so on screen: Funan's own instruments are unknown. Chinese records only say that musicians were sent to the Wu court in 243 CE. The score is a modern evocation, not a reconstruction of Funan music.

**Transitions.**
- Water ripple: a soft splash.
- Lotus unfold: a chime.
- Spreading ink: a soft whoosh.
- A low gong opens every chapter card.
